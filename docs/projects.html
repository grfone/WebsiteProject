Featured Project

🧬 BioResearch-AI

An extensible AI platform for biomedical literature discovery, evidence synthesis and scientific reasoning.

Overview

BioResearch-AI explores how agentic AI systems can assist scientists by combining large language models, retrieval systems, literature analysis and structured reasoning workflows.

Research Motivation

Scientific discovery increasingly depends on navigating thousands of publications, datasets and experimental results. This project investigates AI systems capable of supporting researchers through automated information retrieval, synthesis and hypothesis generation.
